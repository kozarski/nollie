## Subtitle

Helpers for everyday life

## Promotional text

Your own crew of helpers on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. They plan, research and keep track, and anything that sends, buys or posts waits for your yes.

## Description

Nollie gives you a small crew of helpers for everyday life. Ask in a chat, by voice or with Siri, and they plan trips, research, keep an eye on things and get work done on your Mac while you are out.

You stay in charge.
• Sending, buying, posting and deleting always wait for your yes. So do new or changed calendar events, reminders and contacts.
• Face ID checks it's you before a helper pays, sends or posts.
• Papertrail shows what went online and why. The weekly note sums up what each helper did, with one tap to pause or turn one off.
• Night shift tells you what ran while you slept, with a "Why?" on every entry.

Everywhere you are.
• Chats work like Messages, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
• Apple Watch shows who is working, lets you answer approvals and has a big Stop.
• Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center can ask Nollie something or stop every helper at once.

Ready when you are.
• Add a Planner, Shopper, Researcher, Trip planner, Event scout or Home admin in one tap.
• See and edit what each helper remembers.
• Saved cards stay in your Mac's Keychain. Helpers only ever see "Visa ending 4242", and every payment asks you.

Nollie runs Hermes Agent on your own Mac. The iPhone app connects to it.
