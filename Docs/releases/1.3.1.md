Nollie 1.3.1 asks before a helper's words first go to an online model, and names the company that gets them.

**One version number for the Mac, iPhone and Watch apps.** This is 1.3.1 for all of them. Use the apps from the same release.

**What's new**
- **You choose who sees your words.** The first time a helper would send your words to an online model, Nollie names the company, such as OpenAI or Anthropic, and lists what goes: what you write and add, what the helper reads for you, and what it remembers. Nothing goes until you tap Allow. A model on your Mac never needs asking.
- **Take it back any time.** Settings › Privacy › Online models shows each company you allowed. Turn one off and Nollie asks again before the next message goes there.
- **A clearer privacy policy.** nollie.app/privacy now lists every company an online model can belong to, what each gets and why.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** If you already use an online model, Nollie asks once on each of your devices before your next message. The iPhone app is on TestFlight: https://testflight.apple.com/join/2R49Unn5. Version 1.3.1 reaches testers once Apple's beta review finishes.

**Checksums.** Compare `shasum -a 256 Nollie.dmg` with the number in Nollie.dmg.sha256 beside it. They should match exactly.
