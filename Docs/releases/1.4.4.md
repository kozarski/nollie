Nollie 1.4.4 fixes a helper's switches in the demo and makes setup clearer in the Mac App Store version.

**What's better**
- **Setup that fits the App Store version.** That version connects to an assistant you already run, so setup now starts there. It no longer offers Start new or Import, which need the download from nollie.app.
- **A helper's switches work in the demo.** In Try a demo, a helper's Apps tab said "Couldn't check these" under Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and Can pay with my cards. They load now, and on a Mac turning one on asks macOS first, as it does with your own assistant.
- **The demo stays in reach.** Try a demo is on the connection step and on the connection form, for anyone without an assistant yet.

**For the download from nollie.app.** Nothing changes in how it works. 1.4.4 keeps both versions on the same number.

**For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.** Nollie 1.4 works with this release.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** Nollie for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch is on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/nollie-app/id6825756639

**Checksums.** Compare `shasum -a 256 Nollie.dmg` with the number in Nollie.dmg.sha256 beside it. They should match exactly.
