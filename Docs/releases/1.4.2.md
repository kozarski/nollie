Nollie 1.4.2 tidies setup on the Mac and gives the download a cleaner window.

**What's better**
- **Quiet buttons stay quiet.** In setup and Quick question, small buttons such as Finish setup later and What's installed turned black when Nollie's window was in front. They're clear glass again.
- **Set up again starts at the beginning.** If your assistant isn't on this Mac any more, Set up again now opens setup at its first step, with your name and earlier choices already filled in.
- **A cleaner download.** The disk image opens to one window: Nollie, Applications, and Drag to install.

**For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.** Nollie 1.4 works with this release.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** Nollie for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch is on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/nollie-app/id6825756639

**Checksums.** Compare `shasum -a 256 Nollie.dmg` with the number in Nollie.dmg.sha256 beside it. They should match exactly.
