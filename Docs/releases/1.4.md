Nollie 1.4 runs on Hermes 0.23: you can see which helper is working on what, wherever the work started, and Stop ends a run that's going. A model on your Mac and a linked iPhone are easier to set up, too.

**One version number for the Mac, iPhone and Watch apps.** This is 1.4 for all of them. Use the apps from the same release.

**What's new**
- **See who's working.** A schedule or a Board task running on your Mac shows on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch: the helper, what it's on, and for how long. Private bots never show.
- **Stop means stop.** Stop ends a schedule's run that's going right now, from the schedule's page or the bar at the bottom of your chats. The schedule stays on.
- **Missed while your Mac was asleep.** Each schedule has a switch: run once when your Mac wakes, or wait for next time.
- **Keep skills as they are.** One switch per helper stops it rewriting its own skills. It can still use them, and new ones it makes stay as made.
- **What it costs.** The weekly note says about how much went on models this week. A paid tool's price shows on its Ask first card, with what this chat has used.
- **A model on your Mac, made simple.** The model sheet offers Quick start and Best for this Mac, each with its size and download time, and More models for the rest. Nollie checks the free space first, so a model that won't fit is never started. A download shows the time left, pauses and resumes, and setup's model step shows it while it runs. On a Mac with less than 16 GB of memory, setup suggests a plan, since it will feel faster.
- **Listen to any reply.** Every finished reply can be read aloud, not just the last one. Touch and hold a reply, or Control-click it on your Mac, and choose Listen. On the Mac, play also shows when you point at a reply.
- **Papertrail sees helpers' own computers.** It lists what a helper's own computer connects to through Nollie: the address and the time, never what was sent. A computer made before 1.4 joins in once it's reset, or turned off and on.
- **iPhone & iPad in Settings.** Settings on your Mac has a row for it, showing the linked device or Not linked, and the code to link one.

**What's better**
- **A newer engine.** Nollie for Mac moves your setup to Hermes 0.23.1 by itself the first time it opens. Your bots, chats, schedules and settings come along. Nollie's own changes to Hermes are down from four to three, because Hermes now keeps automated check-ins quiet itself.
- **Your assistant looks after itself.** It comes back by itself after Nollie updates, and an update of its parts that was cut short carries on from where it stopped. A busy moment no longer restarts it: only one that has gone, or stayed silent for 45 seconds. Stopping it stops everything it started. While it starts, setup and the model sheet say Starting your assistant… and offer Try again only if it doesn't come back. If its setup isn't on your Mac any more, Nollie says so and offers Set up again.
- **Linking an iPhone.** On some Macs the pairing code couldn't be made: a Mac named with capital letters, or one where macOS hadn't allowed Local Network yet. Both make a code now. With Nollie 1.4 on your iPhone, scanning the Mac's code with the Camera opens it in Nollie. A code that ran out says why, with Scan again.
- **Ask first reads web pages step by step.** Hermes 0.23 works a web page in one go. Ask first reads every step first, and asks before anything that sends, buys, posts or signs in. A step it can't read always asks.
- **Private bots stay private, in more places.** A private bot can't be exported, imported, renamed or used to make a new bot.
- **Chats behave.** Renaming, archiving and deleting a chat works on Hermes 0.23, and a change that's turned down says why. A reply that arrives twice shows once. A message waiting behind another chat says Waiting in line.
- **Questions wait for you.** A helper's question still waits an hour for your answer, unless you chose your own wait.
- **From your iPhone.** A linked iPhone can now remove a schedule and forget a memory note.
- **Night shift from the start.** Night shift is on in a new setup, so the first morning has its card. A setup made before keeps what it had.
- **Setup.** On the Mac, setup shows only what you can act on, and says plainly what went wrong. A download that stops moving gives up after a minute, so Try again shows. On iPhone and iPad, every step fits a small iPhone and the largest text sizes.
- **No camera on the Mac.** The Mac app no longer asks for camera access, which it never used.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.
