Nollie 1.4 runs on Hermes 0.23: you can see which helper is working on what, wherever the work started, and Stop ends a run that's going.

**One version number for the Mac, iPhone and Watch apps.** This is 1.4 for all of them. Use the apps from the same release.

**What's new**
- **See who's working.** A schedule or a Board task running on your Mac shows on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch: the helper, what it's on, and for how long. Private bots never show.
- **Stop means stop.** Stop ends a schedule's run that's going right now, from the schedule's page or the bar at the bottom of your chats. The schedule stays on.
- **Missed while your Mac was asleep.** Each schedule has a switch: run once when your Mac wakes, or wait for next time.
- **Keep skills as they are.** One switch per helper stops it rewriting its own skills. It can still use them, and new ones it makes stay as made.
- **What it costs.** The weekly note says about how much went on models this week. A paid tool's price shows on its Ask first card, with what this chat has used.

**What's better**
- **A newer engine.** Nollie for Mac moves your setup to Hermes 0.23.1 by itself the first time it opens. Your bots, chats, schedules and settings come along. Nollie's own changes to Hermes are down from four to three, because Hermes now keeps automated check-ins quiet itself.
- **Ask first reads web pages step by step.** Hermes 0.23 works a web page in one go. Ask first reads every step first, and asks before anything that sends, buys, posts or signs in. A step it can't read always asks.
- **Private bots stay private, in more places.** A private bot can't be exported, imported, renamed or used to make a new bot.
- **Chats behave.** Renaming, archiving and deleting a chat works on Hermes 0.23, and a change that's turned down says why. A reply that arrives twice shows once. A message waiting behind another chat says Waiting in line.
- **Questions wait for you.** A helper's question still waits an hour for your answer, unless you chose your own wait.
- **From your iPhone.** A linked iPhone can now remove a schedule and forget a memory note.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** Nollie for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch is on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/nollie-app/id6825756639
