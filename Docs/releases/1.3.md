Nollie 1.3 makes mornings and approvals feel calmer. What your helpers did overnight greets you at the top of your chat, a yes feels answered under your finger, and everything reads well at every text size.

**One version number for the Mac, iPhone and Watch apps.** This is 1.3 for all of them. Use the apps from the same release.

**What's new**
- **Your night, in the morning.** What Night shift did overnight sits at the top of your main chat as a small card. Open it to see each step and why, or put it away until tomorrow.
- **Night shift on or off.** Turn Night shift on or off in Settings, from any of your own devices. Someone you share a helper with can't change it.
- **Approvals that feel answered.** A waiting card rises in gently, a yes ticks and a no taps under your finger, and the chat says Needs your OK while it waits.
- **Your wrist feels it too.** Apple Watch taps for each yes, no and Stop, and buzzes when your phone is out of reach.

**What's better**
- **Every text size.** At the largest sizes, names stay whole, buttons keep their room and rows stack instead of squeezing.
- **Easier to read.** Bright titles and tinted buttons in Settings are a touch deeper, and the weekly note gives its dates a line of their own.
- **Addresses break cleanly.** Web addresses and hosts wrap after a dot or slash, never with a hyphen.
- **Fixed.** On a busy phone, a live picture of your Mac could say it dropped when it had really been stopped. It now always says why it ended.
- **Fixed.** Jumping to the newest messages in a long chat now always lands on them.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** Nollie for Mac updates itself and always asks first. The iPhone app is on TestFlight: https://testflight.apple.com/join/2R49Unn5. Version 1.3 reaches testers once Apple's beta review finishes.

**Checksums.** Compare `shasum -a 256 Nollie.dmg` with the number in Nollie.dmg.sha256 beside it. They should match exactly.
