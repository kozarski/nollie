Nollie 1.3.2 makes it easy to get Nollie on your iPhone, straight from your Mac.

**One version number for the Mac, iPhone and Watch apps.** This is 1.3.2 for all of them. Use the apps from the same release.

**What's new**
- **Get the iPhone app from your Mac.** Until an iPhone is linked, pairing starts with a code to scan with your iPhone's camera. It opens Nollie on the App Store. Once it's installed, click I have the app for the pairing code.
- **This Mac.** The foot of the sidebar now says This Mac for Nollie set up on your Mac, instead of a name cut short.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** Nollie for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch is on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/nollie-app/id6825756639

**Checksums.** Compare `shasum -a 256 Nollie.dmg` with the number in Nollie.dmg.sha256 beside it. They should match exactly.
