Nollie 1.3.3 is a tidy-up: your iPhone is clearer about what stays on your Mac, and your Watch shows the Board tasks waiting for you.

**One version number for the Mac, iPhone and Watch apps.** This is 1.3.3 for all of them. Use the apps from the same release.

**What's new**
- **What's new, in the app.** After an update, Nollie tells you what changed and where to find it, with more detail if you want it. You can read it again in Settings, under About.
- **Board tasks on your wrist.** When a task on the Board needs your help, Apple Watch shows it on its own page, with the helper that holds it. You answer on your iPhone.

**What's better**
- **On your Mac, said up front.** An iPhone linked to your Mac can't edit a schedule or change what a helper may use; those stay on the Mac. The iPhone now says so, instead of offering a switch that didn't work. New schedules from the iPhone go through Nollie's chat.
- **Nollie Computer, updated.** The part of the Mac app that lets a helper use the apps you approve is built from Cua Driver 0.46.1.

**Requirements.** A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. For the phone app, an iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. For the Watch app, watchOS 26 or later.

**Good to know.** Nollie for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch is on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/nollie-app/id6825756639

**Checksums.** Compare `shasum -a 256 Nollie.dmg` with the number in Nollie.dmg.sha256 beside it. They should match exactly.
