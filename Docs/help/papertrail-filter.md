# Stop what Papertrail can't see

[← Nollie](../../README.md) · [Help](README.md)

Papertrail shows what left your Mac: when, and where to, never your words. It sees everything your helpers send
through Nollie's door. A few programs go around the door, and Papertrail can't see those. This switch stops them.

It's only in the Mac you download from nollie.app. The Mac App Store edition, iPhone and iPad don't have it.

## Turn it on

1. On your Mac, open **Settings › What left your Mac**.
2. Turn on **Stop what Papertrail can't see**.
3. macOS asks twice. Nollie never answers for you:
   1. It says **Allow Nollie Papertrail in System Settings › Login Items & Extensions.** Press **Open System
      Settings** and allow it there.
   2. Then **Choose Allow when your Mac asks about filtering.**
4. When it's on, it says **Anything a helper starts that tries to go around Papertrail is stopped.**

If you choose **Don't Allow**, everything stays off.

If it says **Not in this version of Nollie yet.**, this copy of Nollie doesn't carry it.

## Turn it off

Turn off **Stop what Papertrail can't see**. Your helpers' programs can go around the door again. While it's off,
your weekly note says **Papertrail's filter is off, so a program that skips Nollie's door could go unseen.**
