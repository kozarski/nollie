# Your week

[← Nollie](../../README.md) · [Help](README.md)

Once a week, a short note says what each helper did and what left your Mac, with a tap beside each line to undo
it or turn it off. It only says what Nollie recorded. Nothing in it is written by a model.

## When it comes

On Sunday evenings at six, unless you choose another day. It comes as a notification, **Your week**, and as a
small card at the top of Nollie's main chat. Tap the card to open the note. Its cross puts it away until next
week's.

Change it in **Settings › Your week**: turn **Weekly note** off, or pick a **Day**. The choice is the same on
every device. **This week** opens the note at any time.

You can read the note on iPhone, iPad and Mac. On Apple Watch there's a page with the week in a few lines; the
whole note, with its taps, is on your iPhone.

## What it says

- **New this week**: a place or service a helper used for the first time, a new schedule, or a helper that asked
  you more than usual.
- **Each helper**: its schedules and how often they ran, its Board tasks, what it asked you and how you answered,
  how often it went online, how many replies stayed on your Mac, and where things went.
- Helpers with nothing recorded are named on one line.

If the week couldn't be read, the note says so. It never guesses.

## The taps

| Beside | Tap | Undo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A schedule | **Pause** | **Resume** |
| A new schedule | **Remove**, after you confirm | None |
| A connected app | **Turn off** | **Turn on** |
| A connected app | **Remove**, after you confirm | None |
| Web search or pages | **Turn off web** | **Turn on web** |
| **Can use your Mac** | **Turn off** | **Turn on** |
| A helper's name | **Mute** | **Unmute** |

Changes to apps and the web reach the helper from its next chat.

**Mute** stops banners for that helper's finished replies, on every device. Its questions, approvals and "started
using your Mac" still come, so nothing that needs you waits unseen.

## Private helpers and shared helpers

Private helpers are never read or counted. Someone you share a helper with never sees your note, its card or its
setting.
