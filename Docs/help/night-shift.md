# Night shift

[← Nollie](../../README.md) · [Help](README.md)

Each morning, Night shift says what your helpers did overnight while nobody was watching: every schedule and
Board task that ran, what each one did, and why it was allowed or what stopped it. A quiet night says **Quiet
night. Nothing ran.**

## Where to find it

- **In Nollie's main chat.** A small card at the top shows the night in a few words. Tap it for the whole Night
  shift. Its cross puts it away until the next morning.
- **Settings › Night shift**, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- **On Apple Watch**, the last Night shift, in a few lines.

## Why?

Beside each step there's **Why?**. Tap it to see who did it and for what, then the rule that let it happen, or
what stopped it. If Nollie didn't keep the reason, it says so.

## Share a picture

The share button in the corner makes a picture of the night. Personal details are blurred, and nothing is sent
until you pick where.

## Turn it on or off

Night shift starts off. Turn it on with the **Night shift** switch in **Settings › Night shift**, on your Mac, iPhone
or iPad. While it's off, the page says **Off**, and there's no morning card.

Only you can turn it on or off. Someone you share a helper with never sees the switch.
