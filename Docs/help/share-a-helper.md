# Share a helper

[← Nollie](../../README.md) · [Help](README.md)

Someone at home, a partner, a parent or a teenager, can ask one of your helpers things from their own iPhone. You
stay in charge: anything that needs a yes comes to you, and you can take it away in one tap.

You need the Mac you download from nollie.app (not the Mac App Store edition). You can share from that Mac, or
from an iPhone or iPad you linked to it.

## What they get

Each person gets their own copy of the helper, with the same look and the instructions you wrote, and none of
your memory or chats. If those instructions hold something personal, edit the copy's instructions after sharing.

| They can | Only you |
| :--- | :--- |
| Chat with the helper | Say yes |
| Send photos | Your memory |
| | Your cards and sign-ins |
| | Your other helpers |

Their copy can look things up and read pages. It can't use this Mac's files, apps or screen, run code, or reach
your calendar, reminders, contacts, connected accounts, saved cards or sign-ins. You can still edit the copy like
any other helper on your Mac.

Private helpers can't be shared.

## Share it

1. Open the helper's menu and choose **Share…**.
2. Press **Add someone**, type their name, then **Make code**.
3. On their iPhone, at home, they open Nollie, choose **Scan QR code** and scan it. They see who is sharing which
   helper, and press **Join**.
4. Your screen says they joined.

The code works once, for two minutes. If it runs out, press **New code**.

## When they need a yes

Their card says **Waiting for** your name, with what the helper wants to do, and one button: **Never mind**.

On your Mac, and on an iPhone or iPad you linked, the ask shows at the top of your helpers list: **Sam asked
Scout**, with **Allow once** and **Deny**. Nollie asks for Face ID before a yes, where it would in a chat.

## What they see

One quiet screen, **Shared with you**, with each helper you shared, a new chat button and the chats they started.
No Board, Settings, Papertrail, schedules or memory. Their helpers work at home only, never through iCloud or
Tailscale.

## Stop sharing

Open the helper's menu, **Share…**, and press **Remove** beside their name. They're cut off at once, and anything
they were waiting on is answered no. Their copy of the helper stays on your Mac with its chats, and you can delete
it like any other helper.
