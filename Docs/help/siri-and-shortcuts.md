# Siri and Shortcuts

[← Nollie](../../README.md) · [Help](README.md)

Nollie works with Siri, Spotlight, the Shortcuts app, Control Center and the Action button, with nothing to set
up. **Settings › Siri & Shortcuts** shows what to say.

## What to say

- **"Ask Nollie"**: Siri asks what you'd like to ask, sends it to your usual helper and reads you the reply. Say
  **"Ask Scout in Nollie"** to ask a helper by name.
- **"What is Nollie doing"**: who is working, and whether anything waits for you.
- **"Stop Nollie"**: ends every task, stops replies and pauses schedules.
- **"New chat in Nollie"**, or **"New chat with Scout in Nollie"**.
- **"Open my Travel chat in Nollie"**: opens a recent chat by its name.

Your iPhone or iPad needs to be unlocked for Ask Nollie and Stop.

## Ask Nollie

The question goes into your helper's main chat, so the whole conversation is there in Nollie afterwards. In
Shortcuts, the reply is the action's result, so you can pass it on.

- If your helper needs your yes to go on, Siri says so and Nollie opens at that chat. Siri never answers it for
  you.
- If your helper takes longer than Siri waits, Siri says it's still on it, and the reply lands in the chat.
- Private helpers can't be asked from Siri. Ask them in Nollie.

Away from home, Ask Nollie needs Tailscale. Through iCloud, use the chat instead.
[Use Nollie away from home](away-from-home.md)

## Stop

**"Stop Nollie"** is the same Stop as everywhere else. It's also:

- **Stop all helpers**, a control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button on iPhone, and Control
  Center and the menu bar on the Mac.
- On the iPhone's Lock Screen while a helper is working. Turn this off with **Show Stop on the Lock Screen while a
  helper is working**, in **Settings › Siri & Shortcuts**.
- **Stop Nollie** on Apple Watch, in Control Center, the Smart Stack and the Action button.

From your iPhone, Stop goes to your Mac at home, with Tailscale, or through your own iCloud.

## The Ask Nollie control

**Ask Nollie** is also a control for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button on iPhone, and Control
Center and the menu bar on the Mac. One tap opens your main chat, ready to type.

## Answer approvals by voice

Off unless you turn it on, in **Settings › Siri & Shortcuts › Advanced › Answer approvals by voice**. Then say
**"Answer the approval in Nollie"**. Siri reads the approval to you and checks before it answers. It only answers
when exactly one approval is waiting.

Sign-ins, and anything that needs Face ID, still need you in Nollie.

## Spotlight

**Show chats in Spotlight** lets you find chats by name from Spotlight. It's on at first on iPhone and iPad, and
off on the Mac.
