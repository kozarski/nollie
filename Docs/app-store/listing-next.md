## Description, iPhone and iPad

Your crew. Your call.

Nollie is a crew of helpers for the jobs you keep putting off: planning a busy week, finding and comparing things to buy, reading up on a question, sorting out a bill or a renewal. They work on your Mac, and you check in from your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

You have the final say. You can see what each helper is doing while it works, and nothing is sent, bought, posted or deleted until you say yes.

On your iPhone and iPad
• Chat with each helper by typing or by voice, and hear replies read aloud.
• Answer approvals from the Lock Screen or your Apple Watch, with Face ID before anything is paid for or sent.
• Stop any job from your iPhone.
• Read Night shift, a note each morning on what your crew did overnight, and Papertrail, which shows what left your Mac and why.
• See and edit what each helper remembers about you.
• Add a ready-made helper in one tap: Planner, Shopper, Researcher, Trip planner, Event scout or Home admin.

On iPad and iPhone Duo, your helpers sit in a sidebar beside the chat.

Your words, your choice
Helpers think with the model you pick: one that runs on your Mac, or an online model under your own account. Before anything first goes to an online model, Nollie names the company, shows what it would get and waits for you to allow it. Settings › Privacy › Online models takes a yes back.

Nollie for iPhone connects to Nollie on your Mac. Tap Try a demo on the first screen to look around first, with nothing to set up.

No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Nollie is open source under the MIT license.
