# Code of conduct

Nollie is a small project made by one person, and it should be a kind place to ask, build and disagree.

## What we ask of everyone

- Be kind and patient. People here have all kinds of experience.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with the idea, not the one who had it.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you've misread something.
- No harassment, insults, threats or unwelcome attention, in any form.
- Keep other people's private details private. That includes chats, keys and screenshots of their
  setup.

## Where it applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when you speak for
Nollie somewhere else.

## If something goes wrong

Corey looks after this repository. He may edit or remove comments, close or lock a thread, and block
someone who keeps breaking these rules.

To report someone's behaviour, use the private form on this repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/kozarski/nollie/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct.
Only Corey sees it, and it stays private.
